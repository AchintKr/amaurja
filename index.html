Smart, local energy sharing for sustainable communities

AmaUrja enables peer-to-peer sharing of surplus renewable energy with real-time monitoring, AI forecasting, and blockchain-secured transactions — lowering costs and reducing waste.

At a glance

Peer-to-peer sharing, AI demand forecasting, and blockchain transaction records — all in one community-focused platform.

  • Real-time energy monitoring
  • Automatic surplus redistribution
  • Emergency power prioritization

Key Features

Peer-to-Peer Sharing

Efficiently route surplus solar energy to neighbors in real time.

AI Forecasting

Predict demand and generation using historical and weather data.

Blockchain Security

Tamper-proof transaction ledger for transparent energy trading.

Emergency Mode

Prioritize essential appliances during outages to keep communities safe.

How It Works

1. Monitor

Smart meters collect generation and consumption data in real time.

2. Match

Surplus energy is matched automatically with nearby demand.

3. Secure & Optimize

Transactions are recorded on-chain and AI optimizes distribution.
